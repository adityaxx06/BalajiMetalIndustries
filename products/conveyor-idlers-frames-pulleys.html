Conveyor Idlers, Frames & Pulleys

Low-runout rollers with precision bearings — sealed for dusty environments in cement, mining and power plants.

Overview

Our idler rollers are made from high-grade ISI-certified pipes and built to handle thrust loads effectively. Fitted with premium quality bearings and a dust-proof sealing system, they ensure smooth operation and long service life in cement, mining, power and ports installations.

Key Features

  • High-grade ISI-certified pipes, built for thrust loads
  • Premium quality bearings for smooth, long-life operation
  • Dust-proof sealing for tough working conditions
  • Idlers, frames and pulleys as matched sets

Range

  • Conveyor idlers — carrying, return and impact duties
  • Conveyor frames — to suit belt width and troughing
  • Conveyor pulleys — head, tail, snub and bend duties

Applications

Cement · Mining · Power · Ports & bulk handling.
