Spring Steel Screen Cloths

Grade-1 spring steel vibrating screen mesh — sourced from Usha Martin / Tata wire — designed for strength, flexibility and long life in the toughest screening conditions.

Overview

Our Spring Steel Grade-1 wire mesh is built for heavy-duty screening in sponge iron, cement, mining and power plants. High resilience and wear resistance keep vibrating screens running reliably with reduced downtime and higher efficiency.

Key Features

  • High strength and durability from Grade-1 spring steel
  • Withstands tough conditions for long service life
  • Manufactured in all standard and tailored dimensions
  • Supplied with clamps or without, to suit installation

Materials

Grade-1 spring steel wire sourced from Usha Martin / Tata. Screen cloths also available in SS 304 and SS 310.

Available Variants

  • With clamps — for quick installation
  • Also available in SS 304 and SS 310
  • Without clamps — as per customer need
  • Standard and customised sizes to drawing

Applications

Sponge iron plants · Cement industries · Mining operations · Power plants.
